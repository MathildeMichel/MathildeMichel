Research interests

My research sits at the intersection of methodological development and ecological applications, focusing on marine bioacoustics and cetacean populations, from large baleen whales to small odontocetes. I develop spatio-temporal statistical models that integrate complementary data sources, such as passive acoustics and visual observations, to better understand cetacean distribution, habitat use, population dynamics and responses to anthropogenic pressures. My goal is to turn these approaches into robust evidence for conservation and management.
